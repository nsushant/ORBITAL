Space logistics and in-orbit servicing

The ORBITAL Project

Optimisation frameworks for on-orbit servicing mission design.

Often without our awareness, our daily lives rely heavily on thousands of satellites orbiting Earth—and this number is rapidly increasing. In addition to Earth-observation missions, satellites play a crucial role in today’s telecommunications networks. They also provide highly accurate time stamps that are essential for verifying and executing financial transactions and maintaining real-time balance in the electrical grid.

As satellite constellations continue to expand, there is a growing need for improved maintenance services. These include replacing old or malfunctioning modules, updating satellite technology and capabilities, and providing refuelling services. Rather than launching every service mission from Earth, governments, space agencies, and industry partners are exploring orbital service centres that could serve as hubs for deploying these missions.

The ORBITAL Project focuses on planning these orbital service centres. We develop methods to determine suitable locations, calculate the required facility capacity and size, establish inventory-replenishment policies, and identify when specific servicing missions should be dispatched.
